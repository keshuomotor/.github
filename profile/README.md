## About Us

Shenzhen Keshuo Motor Co., Ltd. is a micro motor manufacturer established in 2012 and based in Shenzhen, China.

We specialize in permanent magnet stepper motors, hybrid stepper motors, geared stepper motors, micro DC motors, and coreless DC motors for OEM equipment and precision motion applications.

Our engineering and manufacturing capabilities support motor selection, application-specific configurations, and integration into compact electromechanical assemblies.

## Motor Technologies

Our main product categories include:

### Stepper Motors

- **PM Stepper Motors** — Compact permanent magnet stepper motors for positioning and indexing mechanisms.
- **Hybrid Stepper Motors** — Precision stepper motors, including NEMA 8, NEMA 11, and NEMA 17 frame sizes.
- **Micro Stepper Motors** — Miniature stepper designs for space-constrained equipment.
- **Linear Stepper Motors** — Lead screw driven configurations for compact linear positioning.

### Geared Motor Systems

- **Geared Stepper Motors** — Integrated stepper motors with precision gear reduction.
- **Planetary Gear Stepper Motors** — Compact planetary gearbox assemblies for increased output torque.
- **Worm Gear Stepper Motors** — Right-angle geared drive solutions for specialized mechanical layouts.
- **Spur Gear Motors** — Multi-stage spur gear systems for speed reduction and torque conversion.

### DC Motor Systems

- **Micro DC Motors** — Small brushed DC motors for compact electromechanical equipment.
- **DC Gear Motors** — Miniature geared motors, including N10 and N20 configurations.
- **Brushless DC Motors** — Electronically commutated motors for controlled rotary drive applications.
- **Coreless DC Motors** — Low-inertia motors with lightweight rotor designs for fast response and precision motion in compact equipment.
